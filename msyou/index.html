What is this? An independent prototype, dated October 24, 2026. Not an official MSU Mankato, Coalition, or grocery initiative service. The demo never sends or saves your entries on a server. Use fictional examples; don't enter sensitive information.

WHY THIS EXISTS

Organizations have projects.
Projects need crossings.

A neighborhood grocery, a community group, an environmental crew, and a university laboratory may each already hold part of the same solution. We are experimenting with a way to recognize an overlap, invite participation, and preserve the handoff without treating any participant as absorbed by the others.

01 / ENTER

Anyone can arrive

Lightweight participation and readable invitations. A card works on paper as well as on a screen.

02 / CHOOSE

Every receiver keeps authority

Sending is not receiving. Receiving is not accepting. A proposed task is not completed work.

03 / RETURN

The useful trace survives

Know what was proposed, what was actually acknowledged, and what still needs a human.

Read the two-week pilot contract ↗

What success might look like

  1. A participant knows how to offer or ask.
  2. A steward chooses whether to receive it.
  3. A next step has a named, consenting owner.
  4. Anyone can see what is still unresolved—with privacy respected.
  5. The team can decide whether to keep, change, or discard the tool.
All real deployment, contact, identity and retention decisions remain unapproved until the relevant people agree.
