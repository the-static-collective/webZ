THE ACTUAL EXPERIMENT

TWO ROOMS. TWO KEYS. ONE CHOICE.

DEVICE-LOCAL · EXPLICIT FILE TRANSFER

TRAINING / PUBLIC-SAFE ONLY. Proposal text and signed JSON are not encrypted. Do not enter real addresses, vulnerable-person records, case files, private contacts, service credentials or confidential institutional material. An address fingerprint limits who can reply in this demo; it does not hide the content. This is not the encrypted Outbox.

01

GIVE A ROOM ITS OWN KEY

Your browser generates a non-exportable signing key and stores it on this device. A church or campus name does not authorize you to represent it.

Rooms on this device

No device-local rooms yet.

Device loss or cleared browser storage destroys the signing identity: this first experiment does not export private keys. Signed messages remain portable; a room identity is not.

02

WRITE A BOUNDED OFFER

Select the room whose key signs the letter. The message itself remains readable to anyone holding the JSON file.

03

CARRY THE PARCEL

Phone A: download a signed JSON envelope below. Share it directly as a file—Bluetooth, USB, Nearby Share or other deliberate transfer.

Phone B: open this same web application, create a different room, and import the JSON. No online relay is required once the app shell is cached.

Signatures prevent unnoticed changes to the envelope. They do not identify an organization's legitimate spokesperson or protect confidentiality.

Signed parcels on this device

04

THE RECEIVER GETS A VETO

An independent room may sign HOLD, REFUSE or REVIEW. None of these decisions authorizes real-world action.

The sender may import this separately signed receipt, but cannot sign the receiver's key. Key ownership ≠ verified human identity.

Ready. Nothing transmitted, sent, enrolled or authorized.

SUITCASE-002 / THE ORGANIZATION FOUNDRY

EVERY NODE CAN FOUND ANOTHER.

INDEPENDENCE, NOT FRANCHISING

REAL SIGNATURES. SELF-ATTESTED ORGANIZATIONS. Genesis means a locally signed proposal and accepted operating rules; it does not incorporate a legal entity, verify anyone's institutional authority, or publish a public directory. All cards are unencrypted public-safe plaintext; do not use private survivor, client, child, address or contact records. A parent invitation does not authorize or own a child.

05

MAKE A SEED INVITATION

Any room key may offer a method for founding another organization. Export the signed invitation JSON from the parcel desk. The recipient may refuse or ignore it.

A seed is a signed invitation from a key, not permission to operate at a venue or proof of a human identity.

06

FOUND AN INDEPENDENT ROOM

Use a new, separately controlled local room key (create one at step 01). Optionally import a seed from an ancestor. You can also found a root without any ancestor.

07

DISCOVER A VOLUNTARY CAPABILITY

Only nodes with one locally verifiable genesis may offer a public-safe capability. The directory below shows signed files on this device only; there is no central listing, crawl, search service or automatic broadcast.

Discoverable on THIS device

08

WITHDRAW OR VERIFY THE GATES

The original key can sign a withdrawal to distribute to peers. Peers must import the withdrawal to update their own local view; public copies cannot be recalled.

Independent-node evidence (local only)

NODE ALIVE requires actual independently controlled keys and records, a consensual crossing, a valid refusal, a successful offline field test, and real-world stewardship review. Browser records alone cannot certify it.

Nothing published, enrolled or sent by opening this page.

OPEN PRINTABLE SIX-CARD PHYSICAL FOUNDING KIT ↗

How to reproduce: create two distinct keys on two devices. Device A signs and exports a SEED. Device B imports it, creates its own key, accepts rules, and signs a GENESIS. B can then sign a CAPABILITY. A imports the GENESIS and CAPABILITY files, sees the voluntary local listing, and can address an OFFER without forcing delivery or work. B can REFUSE and send back its own signed receipt. Paper copies can carry the same questions when no electronics work.
