CARD 4 / TWO-PHONE FIELD TEST

  1. Open the Suitcase app on two different phones while online; confirm both service workers successfully cache the shell. Record each phone's public key fingerprint separately.
  2. Put both phones offline. Device A signs a seed and transfers JSON intentionally (USB, device file share, QR-to-file adapter if separately reviewed). Device B imports it and checks the fingerprint out of band.
  3. B creates its own key, agrees to rules, signs GENESIS, and transfers the signed JSON to A. A imports it and verifies signature plus seed ancestry.
  4. B optionally signs a public-safe CAPABILITY. A imports that card and addresses a non-sensitive OFFER. B answers REFUSE; A imports the signed response.
  5. Reopen each phone without internet. Confirm readable independent records and the refusal. Record what failed without upgrading a demonstration into a field proof.

CARD 6 / NODE ALIVE GATES

□ New node has accepted rules and owns independent identity/records

□ No ancestor holds its private key or can order it to act

□ Another consenting independently held node verified a signed response

□ The receiver tested a genuine refusal without losing autonomy

□ Operation succeeded with original founder absent and internet disconnected

□ Named actual steward approved the physical venue, people, accessibility and safeguarding

□ Independent field reviewer witnessed the test, failure modes and results

All boxes require human evidence. A web page cannot certify a functioning real-world node.
