The proposed systemic target is a voluntary culture and local practice economy of meals, music, creativity, mutual help and shared material capability operating alongside existing institutions. Starting with people excluded from trusted networks, the six-month experiment tests whether inclusive gatherings and steward listening meetings can lead to a genuine, independently received useful act and a successor table that keeps its own authority.
