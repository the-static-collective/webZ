WIDER HORIZON / NARROW FIRST EXPERIMENT

Build the capacity to begin together, not another compulsory platform.

The systemic target is to remove a hidden prerequisite for cooperation: already knowing someone, holding credentials, or depending on a centralized organization to admit you. The first six-month learning question is smaller: can a qualified, survivor-informed method help an isolated adult reach one real safe next step while preserving refusal, privacy and local control? The method must work without an app, AI, printer or membership.

Prior working software supports signed proposals, separate authority, source-pinned creative demonstrations and reusable instructions. It has not established independent human trust, confidential survivor services, a staffed peer group or community outcomes. Paid lived-experience co-design and qualified safeguarding review are the missing actual relationships; the proposed participants retain the right to reject the method.
