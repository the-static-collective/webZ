One festival makes another.

A traveling song becomes a table. A table becomes a Waseca mini-festival. A mini-festival rehearses a family Commons gathering at Harmony Park. At the big festival, independent teams can invent the next festival together—at Harmony Park, somewhere else, both, or not at all.

Proposals only. None of these gatherings is booked, staffed, funded or affiliated with a venue or Voqal. This page creates a public-safe local draft. It collects no name, address or contact details, makes no network request, and signs no person up.

Four stages. No inherited permit.

What makes a genuine next festival?

A potential successor group chooses its own rules, can refuse a proposed date or venue, acquires independent permission and financing, and can change the inherited program. For an October festival, weather, family accessibility, sound limits, camp operations and the existing Harmony Park calendar must be checked separately. A September event does not authorize October.

We are testing the idea that culture proliferates when a group can inherit a useful method without inheriting command. A real October event is a conditional successor, not a deliverable financed by Voqal's September-ending fellowship.
