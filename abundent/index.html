    A thought, a piece of public text, or a file you select locally. Keep its source.

THE GROUND RULE

A door is a possibility.
Not a claim of possession.

This is a public entrance to distinct experiments, not a single authority over them. External music opens only when selected. Local proposals are not uploads, payments, orders, or published works. The pictured growth rings are illustrative design, not recorded activity.
