LOCAL GROCERY SPOTTED ON FOLDING TABLE

An extremely small beginning. Possibly the beginning of something much bigger.

A POSSIBLE FUTURE · NOT A REPORT OF AN EVENT

WASECA — It started with a farmer and two crates of vegetables.

Someone brought coffee. Someone else brought soup.

A neighbor who couldn't make it got a bag delivered to their door.

A little girl drew a picture of the whole thing, and somebody put it in the newspaper.

By the end of the afternoon, six strangers knew each other's names.

None of this has happened.
But it could.

There's been talk around town about starting a community-owned grocery store. It's a good idea. It takes money, people, planning, and patience.

But what if we started with a table?

What if, instead of waiting until we had everything, we tried doing something with what we already have?

Maybe you grow tomatoes. Maybe you have a pickup truck. Maybe you know somebody who makes wonderful bread.

Maybe you're lonely.

Maybe you're hungry.

Maybe you just like the sound of six strangers becoming neighbors.

The invitation is real.

We're looking for a few people willing to try.

You don't need to join anything. You don't need money. You don't even need a particularly good idea.

Just tell us what you could bring, what you need, or what you'd like to see happen.

Write TABLE → us@abundent.org

If enough neighbors are interested, we'll work out a place and time together. And then we'll print another newspaper.

Only next time, we'll get to write about what really happened.

For now, this is an invitation, not a scheduled event. This is an independent Abundent / Static Collective experiment, not an announcement on behalf of a grocery organization, farmer, or church. Messages are reviewed by a person. Please don't send sensitive details or anyone else's private story. Your name or words won't be published without your permission.
