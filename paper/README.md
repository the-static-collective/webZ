# The Next Paper
The Next Paper replaces the Daily Slice **as the outward-facing invitation newspaper**, beginning with Issue 000 on October 31, 2026. The Daily Slice remains an intact, append-mostly historical witness, not erased or rewritten.

## Editorial promise
- Start with a possible local event, scene, or shared need that a reader can picture in under a minute.
- Clearly distinguish **imagined future**, **real invitation**, **planned activity**, and **reported outcome**. Never present speculative scenes as actual reporting.
- End with a bounded invitation that one willing person can act on without joining a platform.
- Review incoming offers and needs privately; do not imply that sending a message guarantees a match.
- Publish identities, private details, quotes, or photographs only with permission. No invented commitments by other organizations.
- Follow through: a later edition records what happened, what didn't, and what remains open. Keep correction and history visible.
- The measure of circulation is useful exchange and voluntary human participation, not impressions.

The publication is static HTML. The first invitation uses the already established `us@abundent.org` mailbox and an ordinary mailto link. There is no hidden form, mailing-list enrollment, public submission feed, or automated matching.
