01 / MAKE A VIDEO

A song in.
A music video out.

Haunted Toaster is a local-first music-video instrument. Add a finished song, optionally bring artwork and lyrics, explore its visual possibilities, and render a video on your own computer.

Download portable Windows app ↗ Windows installer ↗

Windows x64 · about 212 MB · unsigned prerelease. Windows may warn about an unrecognized app. Obtain the file only from the official GitHub release and inspect it before running. Performance on older hardware varies.

  1. 01Get the app.

    Choose the portable file or installer above. Portable does not require installation.

  2. 02Bring a song.

    Open the app and provide a track. Artwork and lyrics are optional.

  3. 03Make a video.

    Choose the visuals you want and render locally. The current publicly downloadable alpha predates the unreleased beta design.

Original release: August 14, 2026 alpha.8 · Installation notes · Source and development
