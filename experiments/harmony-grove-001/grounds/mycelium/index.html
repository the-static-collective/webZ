04 · RINGS OF ATTENTION

The notebook knows only what was recorded.

No observer-local receipts.

Free Graph's relation grammar and MEMENTO's UNDERSTORY motivated this experimental adapter. The notebook writes to neither repository or authority ledger. Private MEMENTO contents are not copied here.

A forest need not agree with itself.

Different observers can hold different local understandings of the same works. Their paths can coexist without being scored, pooled, or imposed on one another. What matters is the ability to say when a contact occurred, when an old idea became newly relevant, and which authority has—or has not—accepted it.
