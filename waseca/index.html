PLAY IN THE REAL WORLD
What counts as a win?


01
Notice something particular.
One actual need, resource, skill, or question. Be specific without exposing somebody else's private life.


02
Offer a voluntary doorway.
Print a card, talk to somebody, or bring it to a table. Being invited isn't being obligated.


03
Try one useful thing.
Choose a tiny, reversible action with the people who actually have authority to agree.


04
Return with an honest trace.
What happened? What didn't? What kind of steward is missing? A local receipt is not independent verification.

Who is running this?
This is an independent, experimental initiative from Abundent / The Static Collective, beginning in Waseca. It is not an official project of the City of Waseca, any grocery cooperative, food shelf, church, or the Coalition of Care. Relationships with those groups require their own explicit agreement.
Unsubmitted cards are local drafts. Submitted cards are stored privately and are not public until manually reviewed. Published reports are never proof of help delivered. Please do not mistake a copied card for a vetted need or promise.
To explore existing public pathways, visit the Commons, the Garden experiment, or the open source. The separate public question desk is experimental and must not be used for sensitive information.
