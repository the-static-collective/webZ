The operator's promise

Review ≠ verification. Publication ≠ endorsement. A bug report ≠ a person's obligation to fix it. Real people must volunteer; receiving organizations keep their own decisions. If a card contains personal or sensitive information, reject it rather than publish an edited accusation.
