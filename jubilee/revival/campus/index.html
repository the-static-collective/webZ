    04 / THE NEUTRALITY AND CONSENT CHARTER

    The right to remain
    yourself.

    Unconditional care

    Food, shelter, emergency help and support are never conditional on prayer, conversion, church attendance, membership or public testimony.

    Equal dignity

    Believers, nonbelievers and people of other faiths can contribute and benefit. No participant must adopt another's worldview.

    Separate religious space

    Voluntary faith activities stay distinct from public-school activities and publicly funded neutral service; student-led religious expression retains its lawful protections.

    Student agency

    Students participate as co-designers, not recruitment targets, a free-labor pool or a captive audience. Any academic credit or compensation requires an actual institutional agreement.

    Protection of minors

    No direct requests to children, child matching, identifying student profiles, student education records or private locations. School-approved adults, background checks, supervision and guardian consent where required.

    Separate receipts

    Research lead ≠ contact ≠ expressed interest ≠ signed approval ≠ completed act. Each institution independently owns its decisions and may refuse or withdraw.

    For public K–12 schools, the U.S. Department of Education's guidance distinguishes protected private religious expression from government-sponsored religious activity. Local district policy and applicable law also govern any project.

    04 / AUTHORIZE

    Agree

    Approved steward, privacy, resources, permissions and success criterion documented.

    05 / ACT

    Do

    Participants perform a bounded, reversible work with consent.

    06 / WITNESS

    Return

    Evidence, refusal and unknowns preserved; any public testimony separately consented.

    The first honest return can be as small as: “We invited two organizations; one declined, one suggested a different problem, and nobody was served yet.” That is a valid account. A completed act is different and must earn its own witness.
