02 / THE SEVEN-DAY ENGINE

Every Sunday
begins a week.

SUN

Gather

Worship, teach, welcome. Share only consenting public needs and offers.

MON

Hear

Stewards identify one concrete community-level problem.

TUE

Propose

JAR;VIS and PlanZ draft options; humans judge feasibility.

WED

Coordinate

Independent hosts choose whether to cooperate.

THU

Act

Carry out one small, authorized work of care.

FRI

Confirm

Ask what happened, what remains unknown, and who may testify.

SAT

Return

Prepare a meal, song, gathering and honest account for Sunday.

The week is a proposed operating protocol, not an automated program. Helping someone is never conditional on religious participation.

05 / ONE WEEK · ONE ACT

The first invitation.

Not a pledge to join a new institution. A reversible experiment: one church, one existing community organization, one publicly appropriate need, one accountable act.

Acceptance gates: an authorized steward; clear consent; no personal case details on public pages; a bounded task; independent confirmation; permission before testimony or photography; the freedom to refuse.

If the first week produces no completed act, record that accurately. A proposal is not a miracle, an invitation is not an enrollment, and a participating congregation does not surrender its governing authority.

Nothing has been sent or collected.

Draft · to a church leader

What if church didn't end Sunday?

Would your congregation consider a seven-day experiment with one practical act of care, chosen by your own leadership and community?

We would identify one appropriately shareable need, one willing steward, one small action, and one honest account of what actually happened.

You would retain all decisions about participation, doctrine, privacy, volunteers and resources. No money, membership or endorsement is assumed.

Could we talk about one thing worth doing before next Sunday?

06 / THE RULE OF THE ROAD

Participation without capture.

Churches may cooperate without merging. Neighbors may receive help without worshiping. Human beings may decline to be recorded. Local stewards decide what they can actually promise.

THE PARTICULAR SURVIVES EVERY PROJECTION. Source observation ≠ directory listing ≠ verified operating congregation ≠ invitation ≠ accepted partnership ≠ completed act. The technology must never collapse these stages.
