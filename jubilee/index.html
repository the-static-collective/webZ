03 / PRINTABLE FIELD KIT

Two hours.
One useful thing.

Start smaller than a festival. Make the first gathering a rehearsal for lasting community care.

  1. 00–15
    Welcome, food, introductions

    Open to everyone. Help is not conditional on worship, performances or testimony.

  2. 15–35
    Play and listen

    One song from the host community; offer an optional, freely consented answer.

  3. 35–55
    Hear one public need

    A responsible local steward describes one organization-level task.

  4. 55–90
    Do one bounded thing

    Cook, repair, design, research, introduce a resource or recruit a steward.

  5. 90–110
    Witness the actual result

    Keep offers, accepted commitments, refusals and verified work separate.

  6. 110–120
    Send the next song

    Invite the next community to answer and choose its own useful act.

05 / CULTURAL AGREEMENT

Different voices.
Shared repertoire.

Local authority. A receiving community chooses goals and controls its story.

Musical consent. Recording, remix, translation, credit and distribution require creator permissions.

Unconditional care. Services never depend on beliefs, worship or participation.

Receipts over mythology. A contact is not a partner. An offer is not a delivery.

Useful return. Document safe, reusable lessons and leave room for refusal.
