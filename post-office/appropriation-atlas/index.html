POST OFFICE / THE APPROPRIATION ATLAS · 001–008

Borrow the old internet.
Grow something else.

Eight mechanisms worth studying, each with its own falsifiable experiment. This is a public research map, not eight launched services. The first courier experiment is implemented; the others remain distinct, deliberately gated proposals.

01 · EXPERIMENT 004 IMPLEMENTED IN DRAFT

FidoNet-style mailbags

Store, carry, forward. A voluntarily exports a public seed, B holds and reopens it, C reconstructs it after A disappears. The first courier uses manual file handoffs rather than a live message-routing network.

Enter wandering-letter laboratory →

NEXT GATE · independent physical devices, different time and link conditions
02 · HELD RESEARCH

Delay-tolerant bundles

Borrow bounded message lifetimes, custody/forwarding queue semantics, and transport independence from delay-tolerant networking. Test expiry, loss, delayed rejoin, deduplication, and delivery without source uptime. Not a BPv7 implementation yet.

GATE · durable queue, bounded hop budget, explicit consent, expiry policy
03 · HELD RESEARCH

Scuttlebutt-style signed feeds

Creator-owned append-only publishing logs and verifiable signatures. Keep source content distinct from messenger claims. Test key loss, key rotation, fork handling and reader-pinned trust. A hash alone is not a signature.

GATE · human-owned signing keys and verifiable first public feed
04 · HELD RESEARCH

Automerge-style collaborations

Two isolated creators make different changes to branches of the same story or zine, then intentionally reunite them. Keep original source and each authored addition traceable; do not conflate a conflict-free merge with editorial agreement.

GATE · concurrent-edit fixture, explicit merge review, rejection path
05 · HELD RESEARCH

Optical QR postmarks

Use a camera or printed code to transfer small seeds over light instead of radio. For larger files, test sequencing, checksums, missing-frame recovery and accessible noncamera alternatives.

GATE · capacity/error-rate measurement on two physical devices
06 · HELD RESEARCH

WebTorrent music swarms

Creators may optionally distribute specifically licensed songs, zines or media packs across voluntary peers. Seed metadata and rights must be explicit. Never silently enroll visitors into a swarm or expose arbitrary files.

GATE · one consented licensed sample, resource and privacy caps
07 · HELD RESEARCH

Briar-style nearby couriers

Investigate phone-native Bluetooth and local Wi-Fi exchange without a conventional server or cellular connection. This requires supported native/platform capabilities; the static browser page does not create a Bluetooth mesh.

GATE · mobile permission model, nearby discoverability and privacy test
08 · HELD RESEARCH

ActivityPub and AT bridges

Publish the same approved public letter into consenting network-compatible outboxes while retaining the original content-addressed seed and source provenance. Do not automatically copy private replies, followers or identities between networks.

GATE · one owned public test actor/repository, consented outbound only

The ninth idea: a website with a metabolism

Once authored and approved seeds can move safely, let the site propose new indexes and derivative pages. A recommendation is not selection; a projection is not authority; a generated page is not automatically published.

ISSUE → VERIFY → HOLD → HUMAN DISPOSITION → FORWARD → VERIFY → RECONSTRUCT
Source digest ≠ publisher identity. Route hash ≠ signature. Local receipt ≠ globally witnessed custody.
