A letter before the letters.

We've made songs, built strange things, followed unlikely trails, and gathered more unfinished business than any reasonable workshop should contain.

Now we want a way to write to one another without asking an algorithm for permission.

This Post Office belongs to Abundent. The Static Collective is the first voice through the door. The Daily Slice keeps the public record; these letters are invitations to return to it, listen, reply, and make something new.

We play where we arrive.

This is a public preface, not evidence that a mailing list is operational or a message has been delivered. Private replies require separate consent before publication.
