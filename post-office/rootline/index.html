ROOTLINE · STATION A

Plant an encouragement

Write something you would be comfortable being passed to a stranger. Public text only; no names, contact information, personal stories about others, confidential details, or links. A copy can be forwarded beyond your control.

Receive without obligation

Paste the complete JSON seed from someone you chose to hear from, or import its file. Nothing is fetched from your messages, contacts, or other accounts.

The letter that found you

Nothing loaded. No one is waiting for an answer.

HOLD · THERE IS NO SEED TO CARRY

You decide whether to put copied text into an ordinary message. Plain text preserves the human words, not their cryptographic chain. Copy the full JSON or download it if the next person should be able to verify those links. Ordinary SMS is not end-to-end encrypted and can reveal metadata to providers.

Add a little of your own

A new note stays separate from the original; adding never overwrites earlier text.

This first experimental chain holds one origin and at most two voluntary additions. No popularity score, compulsory forwarding, hidden tracker, or public recipient graph.

Underground, not intrusive

There is no recipient registry, contact harvesting, automatic SMS, subscription, persistent local storage, or public directory in this experiment. A recipient can ignore it, keep it, add something independently, or pass it along after choosing a channel. Stopping is a successful outcome.

SHA-256 links check that the text hasn't accidentally changed since the current parcel was created, but a malicious intermediary can replace the content and recompute unsigned hashes. This is not proof of authorship, informed consent by prior authors, anonymity, or secure delivery. Actual person-to-person field testing remains ahead.
