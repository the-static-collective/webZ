Two devices, one public seed, one explicitly approved crossing. The computers exchange WebRTC pairing descriptions manually; if they establish a direct channel, they send only a bounded seed JSON. Abundent never relays the file.

Science boundary. This is experimental WebRTC, not Soulseek/IPFS discovery. Connecting may expose network addresses to the other peer. The optional STUN service can learn your public IP; it is used only if you select it and initiate pairing. There is no TURN relay and connectivity may fail behind some NATs. Pairing codes are manual checks, not authenticated identity or signed authority.
