WORLD CARTRIDGES / TWO INDEPENDENT TIMELINES

What happens when
neighbors cooperate?

Build a tiny fictional village, fork it into the same world under two different policies, then watch scripted neighbors encounter scarcity, refuse help, share food, and build infrastructure. Every fork is kept; neither can rewrite its parent.

Not real people. Not AI models. Twelve deterministic game actors follow simple consent and survival rules. The simulated village is not a Minecraft server, and a content hash is not a signature. No network, accounts, contact, grants, real-world outcomes, or actual Jubilee rewards.

What survived?

These are toy-world measurements, not predictions of how real communities behave. Differences are consequences of the declared policies and scripts.
