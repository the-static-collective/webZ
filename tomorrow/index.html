A different kind of recruitment

When a task needs someone, finding them becomes the next task.

Instead of hiding the missing reviewer behind an administrative bottleneck, we put the opening where people can volunteer for it. Every independent review begins with finding someone willing, qualified for this particular question, and free of relevant conflicts.
