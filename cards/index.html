02 / CARD → ACTION

One card can make a round trip.

  1. Make it.

    Describe one object, need, or capability you actually know about.

  2. Carry it.

    Share a link directly to someone you choose. You can also save JSON or print a paper card.

  3. Hear back.

    They tap “I can help,” “I'm interested,” “Question,” or “Pass,” then send a response link back.

  4. Do the thing.

    Someone acts in the physical world. A person reports what happened; another can confirm or dispute the report.

A link is not permission.

There is no payment, custody tracking, authenticated identity, or legal transfer here. Physical handoffs require their own clear agreement. Human reports remain self-attested.

03 / HAVE A CARD OR RETURN?

Open a card link or file.

If someone sent you a Living Cards link, simply open it. You can also paste it here or import their JSON file. No login needed.

THE FIRST FIELD TEST

One real thing. One person. One return.

Start with something simple and safe: a spare chair, a book, or a small help request. When someone sends back a response, we've tested whether a card actually helped a real person connect with a real possibility. If a physical act occurs, report it; the other person can confirm or dispute it.

What broke? Was it useful? You can send field feedback. Nothing about a card is emailed automatically.
