01 / THE HUMAN WAY THROUGHTHREE CONVERSATIONS ARE PLENTY

No pitch required. Just a door.

01

Offer a question.

“What's something already happening here that could become easier if people found one another?”

02

Let the person decide.

They can talk, decline, take a card, suggest a connection, or leave. No one has to write anything down.

03

Return only what's permitted.

Only with explicit permission, turn a generalized, non-identifying question into a local draft. No names, contacts, addresses, or stories.

03 / LOCAL QUESTION PRESSOPTIONAL · NO ACCOUNT · NO SUBMISSION

Only the question crosses.

A private conversation stays private. This form contains no field for a person's name, phone, location, or private notes. It creates a candidate file on your device only.

A checked box is an operator's statement, not independent verification of another person's consent or an organization's authorization.

Use general language. Never type names, contacts, exact addresses, private circumstances, or specific requests someone has not authorized.

05 / BEFORE YOU GO HOMENO DATA REQUIRED

A return can be a question.

↺

One genuine, consented public question is a successful field test. A person who chooses not to participate is equally respected.

After the gathering: separate what you observed, what a person permitted you to repeat, what you propose, and what a receiving project actually accepted. Do not convert attendance, a card, a conversation, or a graph link into permission.

Field witness checklist — for your own use
  1. Were cards available without sign-in or obligation?
  2. Did participants have an unpressured chance to decline?
  3. Did you receive permission for the exact public-safe wording of any question?
  4. Did you avoid gathering names, phone numbers, addresses and sensitive stories?
  5. Is every resulting file still an UNPUBLISHED CANDIDATE, awaiting a separate source-owner decision?

Record only non-identifying counts if useful. Do not call a story, a need, a relationship, or a receiving organization's consent verified without evidence.
