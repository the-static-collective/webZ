THE STARTING POINT

We already belong to overlapping worlds.

People, artists, growers, organizations, congregations, workplaces, and neighbors share a region without becoming one institution. Their boundaries matter. So do the relationships beneath them.

THE TEST

Does a question survive the crossing?

Choose one public-safe question. Route it to independent capability models. Rehearse separate HOLD, DECLINE, or CONSIDER responses. Bring back a receipt that never claims an action happened.

THE RULE

No assimilation. No invented consent.

Source projects remain independent. Only a human owner can make a real commitment. Here, every response is a simulation and every export stays on your own device.
