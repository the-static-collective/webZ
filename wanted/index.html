THIS IS A DEVICE-LOCAL EXPERIMENT, NOT A MARKETPLACE.

There are no connected buyers, no real-time claim race, no verified funds, no payout, and no public listing. Anything you create stays in this browser. The fictional $20 request is clearly marked as a simulation. Do not exchange money or travel based on it.

01 / BUYER MAKES THE ASK

I will pay $____ for…

Practice describing a real purchase intent. You can copy it for your own use, but it is not published or funded.

02 / FINDER SEES THE MONEY FIRST

From “want” to “got it.”

  1. Buyer sets the deal.Item, acceptable condition, price, area, deadline.
  2. Real funds are verified.A payment provider authorizes them before the word “secured” is used.
  3. Finder taps “I have it.”One short, server-issued reservation; everybody else sees the hold.
  4. Both confirm the exchange.Inspection and handoff are separate from payment settlement.
  5. Money is released by the provider.Availability to the seller depends on provider terms—not a magic instant payout.

WHAT MAKES THE REAL VERSION DIFFERENT?

Not another classifieds feed.
A queue of funded needs.

In the live version, unpaid wants never masquerade as committed purchases. A reservation must be atomic across devices. The buyer's money must really be authorized, and claims, refusals, expiry, disputes, and payouts must each have an independent receipt.

Nothing here has been sold or secured. This experimental door exists to make the next real crossing testable.
