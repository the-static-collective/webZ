IF WE TRIED IT WITH REAL PEOPLE

Three different things called credits.

Buying credits would need genuine, verified funding or authorization; nobody gets a funded listing from typing a number. Scout credits could represent an agreed share of a real reward after a confirmed transaction. Trust credits could record verified contributions without pretending to be spendable money.

The practical experiment is not a platform-issued currency. It's a faster way to expose already-existing buying demand, let multiple people contribute to fulfillment, and use one transparent budget. Money rails and actual receipt authority are separate engineering and legal gates.
