TRUST BOUNDARY

The phone number isn't the signer

A correctly verified signature proves that the embedded public key signed this specific claim. It does not prove the phone number, real creator, copyright, delivery, consent, local file availability, or a reLATTE receipt. Someone may replay a legitimate old postcard. SMS is neither encrypted nor reliably delivered.

The receiving page can verify without contacting Abundent or Suno. The signed claim contains the source URL and submitted-file hash, not audio. Saving or forwarding anything remains a human decision.
