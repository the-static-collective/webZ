A little source note

These four Suno playlists were observed publicly on October 11, 2026. Their creation date of October 6 is creator-declared; Suno's public pages did not independently provide the timestamp.

Suno playlists can change after this snapshot. This page preserves observed titles, identities, sequence, and source links as a curatorial view—not a claim of immutable Suno state or formal LemonPRESS admission.

For source-readable data, open the album/playlist manifest. For the still-open LemonPRESS lineage, see the house proposal and the crawler proposal.
