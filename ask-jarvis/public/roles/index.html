STEWARD SEEDS · EXPERIMENT 021

You don't have to find the steward.
You can help become one.

A role doesn't need an incumbent to begin. It needs somebody curious enough to take one small responsibility, figure out what matters, and invite the next willing person to help.

Experience optional. Titles unnecessary. Authority not implied. A small, reversible experiment first.

THESE ARE OPEN STARTER ROLES, NOT VACANCIES IN AN EXISTING ORGANIZATION. Abundent is independently inviting people to help explore them. Interest is not an appointment, credential, verified volunteer registration or agreement with the grocery cooperative, a church, the Coalition, a park, or another institution. You control whether any contact is sent. Actual permissions, stewardship of a physical place, and commitments must be confirmed by their rightful owners.

The private interest form is an existing Abundent contact desk. It asks for your contact details and explicit permission to email you; we do not auto-submit it. Sharing an invitation does not sign another person up. You can invite one or two people, or someone can pass it along voluntarily. Nothing counts as a confirmed participant without their own yes.

The first useful outcome may be a new question, a corrected plan or a refusal. That's how real steward roles grow—without pretending somebody already holds them.
