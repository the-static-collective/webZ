AFTER THE GATHERING · THE HUMAN RECORD

SO... WHAT HAPPENED?

Don't make a follower list. Write down a little of what happened—and what you might want to do together next. This is a private draft in this page until you explicitly download it. Nothing is sent to Abundent.

No automatic sharing. Downloading notes does not attest to agreement by other participants. Ask before quoting or naming others.

7 DAYS IS A RHYTHM, NOT A REQUIREMENT.

Move the date. Change the host. Branch into several circles. Or let a table end. Nobody becomes a member by receiving a link, and nobody owes the next meeting.
