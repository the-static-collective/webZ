THE REAL BOUNDARY This is an independent Abundent experiment, not an official grocery, Coalition, campus or church service. It uses the existing account-free Seventh Table ledger. People may submit real offers, needs, voluntary expressions of interest and reports to one invitation-scoped room. It does not authenticate identities, verify consent, perform work, share private contact information, message participants, or prove completion. Meet and obtain permission directly; choose safe, lawful work with the people involved.
01 · ENTER WITH AN INVITATION

Have a table? Bring its key.

To begin, a host opens a Living Chair room and privately shares the guest invitation with people interested in one small project. They post their own NEED and OFFER cards in that room. The host reviews cards before other guests can see them.

This page keeps your room key in your browser URL fragment only. Do not forward your private host key. Guests and hosts can both submit notes, but host moderation determines what other guests can see.

03 · COMPOSE

Propose one possible crossing.

Select one visible need and one visible offer. Publish a short proposed plan in this room. This does not contact anyone or authorize work. Guest proposals require host review.

04 · TWO SEPARATE VOICES

Did each person choose this?

A person who actually contributed a NEED or OFFER card may independently express interest in one proposal. These are self-declared statements, not verified identity, binding consent or authorization to do work. Do not submit on someone else's behalf. Any real introduction must be separately agreed to offline.

05 · WITNESS & RETURN

What actually happened?

Report the outcome, including partial progress, a declined plan, or simply unknown. Completion statements remain unverified. A host may review guest reports before they appear. No report causes a payment, broadcast, credit or external action.
