The Campfire.

Nobody has to be appointed before they can care. Pick a question, decide where you'd like to stand, and take one useful, reversible step. Then invite somebody else to help shape what comes next.

Inspired by a real community campfire exercise, but independent of the Coalition. This website doesn't enroll you in the Greater Mankato Coalition of Care, the grocery co-op, a church or any other organization. Being closest to a question is not authority over other people. 01 / WHICH FIRE CALLS TO YOU?

Choose the work worth gathering around.

Every fire is an open Abundent proposal. A theme is not an official program or a claim that anyone is running it.

Or light a new one.

02 / WHERE WOULD YOU STAND?

Your commitment. Your choice.

Stand near the edge to listen, or move closer to try something. You can change positions or walk away at any point. This is about the amount of responsibility you'd like to explore, not rank or formal membership.

Choose your place by the fire.

All four places are welcome.

03 / THE RIGHT TO TRY

Your first useful move.

A seven-day experiment, not a permanent office:

    Pass the fire, not an assignment.

    Invite one or two people to contribute, or ask someone to carry the invitation as an ambassador. Their own choice is the only yes that counts.
