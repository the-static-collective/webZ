    04 / THE LAW OF THE LAND

    A world can grow without inventing results.

    OBSERVATION ≠ EVIDENCE OF IMPROVEMENT
    Data and field sightings stay source-owned and require their own verification.

    PLAY ≠ PERMISSION
    Real cleanup, water monitoring, private land access, hazards and protected sites require responsible human approval.

    SIMULATION ≠ DEED
    Native Full Measure and GrO contracts are independently tested against fictional source packets. No real Deeds, tenet publications or source judgments occur.

    INTEGRITY > CONTINUITY
    Refusal, rest, inability to help physically and uncertainty are welcome outcomes, not failed participants.
