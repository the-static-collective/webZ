  04 / HOW SOMETHING REAL EMERGES
  
    A growing circle, not a vacant office chart.
    
  
    
   
    ONE
    An open need
    We state the missing ingredient. Nobody is counted as recruited.
    
   
    TWO
    A first object
    Someone drafts one safe, public-facing piece of work.
    
   
    THREE
    Voluntary review
    Another willing human changes, refuses, or improves it.
    
   
    FOUR
    A bounded trial
    Only after the specific consent and safety gates are satisfied.
    
   
    FIVE
    Stewardship
    Ongoing responsibility emerges by new agreement, not by an algorithm or seniority.
    
  
    
  
    Non-negotiable gate: No public stranger matching, confidential survivor intake, or unsupervised peer-listener roster until people, safeguarding, reporting, and withdrawal procedures are actually established.
