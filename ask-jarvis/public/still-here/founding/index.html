02 · A bounded rehearsal

A 45-minute founding workshop.

No existing friend or personal network is required. The first rehearsal is conditional: once an independently interested adult chooses to opt in, a human organizer may ask if they want to critique the idea using fictional examples only. Until someone consents, the workshop remains a self-contained packet anyone can read, print, and comment on through the volunteer desk. It is a design exercise, not survivor support, legal advice, therapy or case intake. Anyone may decline or leave.

04 · Covenant awaiting real review

What we would protect.

Belonging is free.

No disclosure, diagnosis, proof, faith statement, service obligation or court position required. Helping later is voluntary, never repayment.

No adversary assembly.

Do not name or investigate co-parents or children, coordinate confrontation, or decide another family's legal case.

No surprise connections.

No recording, screenshots, publishing attendance, unsolicited private messages or introductions without each person's separate consent.

Listening is not adjudication.

Do not diagnose, investigate allegations, direct legal strategy or treat a personal account as a verified finding.

Leave means leave.

Passing, resting, declining, withdrawing and saying no are valid. Confidentiality expectations must be honest about legal and human limits.

Watch the watchmen.

No founder certifies complaints about themself. A separate qualified reviewer and functional removal/response process are prerequisites to live service.

These are proposed safeguards, not a binding participant agreement, protected confidentiality, or proof of independent review. Before a real meeting, qualified local people must check reporting duties, privacy, facilitation, risk and consent.

05 · The first seven days

Build one real capacity.

  1. Day 1: finish the public-safe welcome and open-work pages; once reviewed and published, make them discoverable without requiring a personal introduction.
  2. Days 2–3: allow optional, consent-based volunteer interest; if nobody volunteers, the open invitation remains and no appointment is inferred.
  3. Days 4–5: prepare an independent professional safeguarding-review request, without treating a proposed contact as an agreement.
  4. Days 6–7: revise the public rules from any voluntarily received non-sensitive feedback; otherwise leave the work open and useful, not failed.
Hard opening gate: no survivor group, anonymous matching, case submissions, support-chat room, or live report handling until independent safeguarding review, trained accountable facilitation, safe contact and withdrawal processes, confidentiality limits, and human incident handling are ready. The prototype Watch the Watchmen tool is not independent oversight.
