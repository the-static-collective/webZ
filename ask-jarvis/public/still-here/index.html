DOOR THREE · PEOPLE ARE NOT APPLICATIONS

Help shape the work, without claiming authority.

Someone who can review fictional cases for one afternoon may make the first survivor conversation safer. Someone who checks a source may prevent misleading advice. A person who notices an inaccessible button may make the entire project more usable.

Potential builders and volunteer coordinators can use the existing independent Abundent contact desk to express interest, with explicit contact consent. That desk is not a confidential survivor intake channel. Please do not send identifying stories, case documents, allegations or information about children through it. There are no confirmed survivor-group staff, matched peers or appointments.

When could a real peer group begin?

Not when a page is built or a volunteer clicks “yes.” Before live peer support, the group needs a responsible human operator, confidentiality and data minimization rules, trained/vetted moderators, consent and contact protocols, conflict and abuse-response procedures, boundaries with legal representation, and a tested withdrawal/complaint process. People with lived experience may help shape those rules without disclosing their stories.

What about Still Here, BLACKSTAR and GRAFT?

This page is the early doorway, not a case database. Still Here's private chronology approach informs the blank template. BLACKSTAR and GRAFT are candidate tools for a future privately controlled workflow, but this page does not open their databases, upload records or claim a deployed integration. Approved, de-identified process improvements—not individual stories—could eventually inform the shared project knowledge.
