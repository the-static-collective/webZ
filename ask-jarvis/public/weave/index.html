THE PART THE COMPUTER CAN'T DO

CLOSE THE TRIANGLE IN REAL LIFE.

ASK A. May I introduce you to someone working on a related possibility?

ASK B. May I introduce you to someone who has a related need or skill?

CONNECT. If both say yes, arrange the introduction in their chosen way.

STEP BACK. Invite them to continue directly. Neither needs to join Abundent.

One consent doesn't imply the other. If either declines, respect it. A connection is successful when it can continue without a permanent hub.
