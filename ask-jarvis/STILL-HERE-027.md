# STILL-HERE-027 · Survivor-led first door, no referral maze

**Status:** implementation stacked on STILL-HERE-026, itself an unmerged branch. **Not a staffed service, charity registration, peer network, or production launch.**

## Human commitment

Abundent's first publicly legible community mission begins with fathers who have survived domestic abuse and are navigating isolation, separation, or parenting conflict. No survivor must prove an allegation, disclose identifying details, accept a diagnosis, or agree with a legal theory to use the free first-step tools. Anyone facing isolation can enter.

> You don't have to do this alone. One particular, one action, one return.

## Useful now

1. No-account, no-remote-intake page at `/still-here/` with a one-hour personal plan.
2. In-memory chronology and portable context: differentiate observation, reports, inference, document possession, event time and first source encounter.
3. A user-controlled invitation chain for one **known trusted person**. The page never forwards a private narrative or claims a volunteer is standing by.
4. A parenting question worksheet and optional official court documents (Minnesota court self-help and fee waiver), not institutional crisis-line routing.
5. Source-pinned native Black Star via GRAFT. Only privacy-minimized Ghost Cut candidate export; no browser Python execution or private archive ingestion.
6. Abundent Ask JAR;VIS homepage points visibly at the survivor-first door.

No crisis hotline or crisis-line service is linked from the Still Here page. Physical safety can be discussed without making a crisis intermediary mandatory.

## Safety and consent boundaries

- No public survivor submission, analytics, cookies, browser persistence, or model prompts from the Still Here page.
- Drafts live in page memory. Copy, download, print and mobile share are initiated by the human. Device/account monitoring can still expose activity.
- No verified peer registry, trained moderation, private messaging, emergency monitoring, provider relationship, identity certification, legal representation, or case-outcome advice exists.
- Allegations are not public GRAFT data or material for moderation-free matching or automated publishing.
- Human helpers must consent; narrower, decline, and permission-before-recruitment are normal responses. Never evade no-contact orders by using an intermediary.
- Self-report is not verification. Source-time is not event-time. A valid timeline does not prove an allegation.
- A malicious person may present as a victim or helper. Persistent stranger matching requires trained moderators, consent, blocking, abuse reports, and retention policies before release.

## Next operational gate

Ask two or three willing survivor-informed reviewers, including men with lived experience, to review the text and threat model. Recruit peer listeners only with clear boundaries and appropriate supervision. Do not open unmoderated anonymous stranger matching.
