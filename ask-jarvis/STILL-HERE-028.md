# STILL-HERE-028 · The Right to Try / Survivor Stewardship Campfire

**Owner field:** Abundent / Still Here. **Donor concept:** CAMPFIRE-022 from the same webZ tree. **Baseline:** STILL-HERE-027 (survivor-led first door). This candidate is stacked on 027 and requires independent preview review.

## The particular

A visitor should be able to say *I might help begin this*, choose a distance from the campfire, and produce a small trial without waiting for an organization to conjure an appointed leader.

This is not a request to put untrained strangers in contact with isolated survivors. **The missing stewardship work itself becomes the first invitation.**

## Five open work questions (editorial, not verified vacancies)

- **Safeguarding groundwork:** what would make consent, incident response, reporting, removal, boundaries and human accountability real? This is the **critical predecessor** to stranger matching.
- **Welcome and invitations:** how do arrivals find a helpful first step without being pressured to disclose a case?
- **Peer listening practice:** how could bounded listening work without diagnosing or adjudicating, after qualified review?
- **Practical navigation:** which source-backed public documents and ordinary tasks can be made more legible without unauthorized legal advice?
- **Tools and accessibility:** can an older phone use the local-first instruments without leakage, false promises, or stored allegations?

## Campfire reuse, not another role hierarchy

The specialized `/still-here/start/` page imports the existing CAMPFIRE-022 `CIRCLES`: curious, helper, starter, coordinator. None constitutes appointment. A seven-day role × standing-specific plan creates one **public-safe first object**, invites one or two consenting reviewers, and returns a local unsigned self-report. It accepts pause and refusal, not just completions.

Proposed lifecycle:

```text
OPEN NEED
  → VOLUNTARY CHOICE
  → PUBLIC-SAFE FIRST OBJECT
  → CONSENTED REVIEW
  → [HOLD until safeguarding, permission, accountable stewards]
  → BOUNDED TRIAL
  → EXPLICITLY AGREED STEWARDSHIP (if earned and desired)
```

Selection never changes a person's role in the organization. Trials do not automatically graduate, qualify, or give access to others.

## First actual organizational participation seam

A person who *explicitly* clicks **Open optional private interest form** crosses to Abundent's existing private `/contact/` desk. A strict three-field allowlisted fragment carries only the public role ID and standing ID. It never carries identity, experiences, survivor cases, notes, phone numbers, or an assertion of authority.

The existing contact form preselects its current `volunteer` category and a fixed non-identifying public-safe note. The form separately requests email, contact permission and a human **Send** action. It uses its existing private storage and no new public community posting. No background submission or partner recruitment occurs; submission is interest only, not guaranteed reply or staffing. Do **not** submit case material to this general-interest desk.

## Safeguards and opening conditions

No staffed service, checked credentials, live listener matching, secret case archive, moderator dashboard, automatic open-needs roster, or emergency monitoring is created. Refusal and stepping away are allowed and explicitly surfaced. No hotline referrals added.

Real stranger matching waits for at least:
1. An accountable and qualified human safety review, including male survivor voices and possible impersonation by abusers.
2. Moderation boundaries, a confidential incident report pathway, block/withdrawal and revocation controls.
3. A real privacy/retention design for any peer communication, including threat models for monitored devices.
4. Appropriate reviewer training, non-adjudication rules, and explicit informed consent by both sides.
5. Measured small field trials under separate approvals.

GRAFT may hold only owner-reviewed public lessons about the *method*; no personal survivor testimony is ingested automatically.
