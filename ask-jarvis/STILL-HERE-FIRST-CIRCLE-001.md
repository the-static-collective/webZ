# STILL HERE · FIRST CIRCLE 001
**Founding workshop / operational packet · 27 October 2026 · DRAFT / UNSTAFFED**

> Build the conditions in which people can safely stand beside one another. Do not solicit painful stories to prove a need.

## What exists and what does not

The **Porch** is a public, no-intake first step. **Seven Days of Returning** is a voluntary private practice. **The Right to Try** offers public-safe work proposals. Those pieces are prototypes and do **not** constitute a functioning survivor support service.

The proposed **Still Here Circle** begins with fathers experiencing isolation, harmful relationship dynamics and difficult co-parenting, without excluding survivors of other genders. The group is intended for peer companionship, practical stability, mutual dignity and optional community contribution. It is **not** a court strategy group, a fact-finding tribunal, therapy, legal representation, a custody campaign or a place to exchange allegations about identified people.

## The first experiment: a *founding workshop*, not a survivor group

**Length:** 45 minutes only if an independent adult voluntarily opts into the rehearsal. **Participants:** founder plus a consenting volunteer reviewer, who need not know the founder personally; an independent safeguarding professional should separately examine readiness. The prework can be inspected or printed by anyone without enrolling. **Inputs:** this public protocol and **fictional examples only**. No survivor stories, no custody details, no names of family members, no private evidence, no recording. There is no personal-friend prerequisite. If no volunteer appears, the founding packet remains useful and the session does not occur.

## Draft Circle covenant — for qualified review, NOT active admission

1. **Choice and dignity.** No one must disclose trauma, diagnosis, proof, spiritual belief, legal position, service hours or family details to earn respect. Attending never creates volunteer debt.
2. **No adversary assembly.** Do not name, investigate, publicly accuse, contact, locate or coordinate action against another participant's partner, co-parent, child, judge, worker or alleged abuser.
3. **Consent at every crossing.** Do not exchange phone numbers, identities or make introductions without each person's separate choice. No surprise follow-up, photography, screenshots, recordings or AI transcription.
4. **Speak from experience, not authority.** Listening is not diagnosing, prescribing treatment, directing custody cases, deciding who is truthful, or giving individualized legal advice.
5. **Participation is reversible.** Passing, silence, taking a break, leaving, withdrawing from optional work and declining follow-up are valid; do not publish attendance or count it as accomplishment.
6. **Confidentiality is a norm, not an absolute guarantee.** Other attendees may breach it; operator and platform metadata may exist; legal duties and limits require locally competent review before live meetings. Never promise privilege or sealed secrecy.
7. **Power must be reviewable.** Founder/moderator cannot independently dispose of concerns about themselves. A separate willing qualified human must own conflict reviews. Participants need a practical way to report, be heard, stop contact and request deletion where applicable.
8. **Minimize custody.** No central archive of survivor testimony, child details, court records or abuse allegations. Black Star/GRAFT evidence remains separately controlled and private; public GRAFT may only accept explicitly approved method-level lessons.
9. **No forced faith or recovery doctrine.** Spiritual support may be offered only when specifically invited; the group remains usable without religious participation.
10. **Do no harm before growth.** If appropriate staff, protections or permissions are absent, keep the activity at public-safe *planning* rather than inviting strangers into an unmoderated room.

For research and expert review, see NNEDV Safety Net's technology and confidentiality guidance (https://nnedv.org/content/technology-safety/) and Rutgers Center for Research on Ending Violence's group-facilitator guidance (https://socialwork.rutgers.edu/centers/center-research-ending-violence/resource-hub/support-groups). These are educational references, **not participating partners**.

## Real support-group launch gate: ALL required

A live, invitation-only trial with adults occurs **only after** the following are verified by real people, independently where necessary:

- [ ] A second consenting, conflict-independent adult is available and understands the limits of their role.
- [ ] An appropriately experienced survivor-support practitioner has examined and corrected the safeguarding protocol, with attention to male survivor inclusion, coercion and impersonation.
- [ ] An accountable facilitator, preferably with a co-facilitator, has appropriate training, scope and cover for a group involving abuse survivors.
- [ ] Eligibility, identity-privacy, entry, disruption, removal, blocked-contact, no-retaliation and accessible withdrawal processes are documented and rehearsed.
- [ ] There is a confidential **human-operated** process concern and urgent safety response pathway with disclosed response limits; no experimental `/watch` database is represented as real external oversight.
- [ ] Safest available location/meeting technology and methods of contact are chosen case-by-case; online/monitored-device threat modeling, metadata, consent, accessibility, retention and deletion are understood. No public meeting roster or exact location.
- [ ] Confidentiality's legal limits, reporting duties, insurance/risk and organizational form receive appropriate local review. No claims of legal privilege.
- [ ] The entire invitation and participant agreement are tested using fictional scenarios; people can opt in, decline, withdraw and correct information without punishment.
- [ ] The planned first adult meeting has a bounded capacity, no recording, no child participation, no public searchable chat history, a facilitator, and a safe cancellation/hold path.

**Any unchecked item = no live survivor circle.** This does **not** prevent the no-intake Porch, individual local practice, fictional rehearsal, or independently requested professional advice.

## An invitation that can be found without knowing anyone

The founder currently does **not** have a trusted person to invite. That is a design constraint, not a failure of the founder or a reason to demand more unpaid social labor. Our initial distribution strategy is **build the no-intake Porch and a public-safe open invitation**, then give strangers a way to offer *bounded, non-sensitive* help at a distance. A contact-desk submission is a prospect's explicit request to be contacted; do not publish their identity, forward their message to other volunteers, or treat it as proof they are vetted.

1. **Useful without participation:** the no-account Porch, printable founding rules, local private exercises, and clear progress status must work for someone who arrives alone; no human responder is implied.
2. **Open roles rather than private introductions:** post clear, inspectable, accessible needs—safeguarding critique, fictional scenario review, proofreading, accessibility, steward accountability. Prospective contributors may join the work without needing to share a survivor identity.
3. **One bounded opt-in:** a reader can choose a role in `/still-here/start/`, then *separately* open the existing `/contact/` volunteer desk, provide an email, affirm permission for reply and explicitly send. Do not accept abuse narratives at this desk. A submitted interest is not an application, appointment, a secure service request, or guaranteed reply.
4. **Incoming work stays on HOLD:** no automated match or distribution, no survivor-member directory, no processing unmoderated disclosures, no implicit follower, no child records. Humans must review requests before any introduction or role assignment.
5. **Discovery once released:** link public-safe welcome and helping doors from Abundent; publish a poster/short invitation only when its landing page really works and has been checked. No dependence on the founder's existing friends and no invented reach/engagement metrics. Avoid implying affiliations with churches, agencies, advocates or local organizations who have not agreed.
6. **Growth gate:** a public page is allowed; opening a confidential survivor group still requires independent qualified safeguards, trained facilitators, separate complaints handling and consent/withdrawal procedures.

**The group cannot demand connections from people whose isolation is part of the reason the group must exist.**

## What belongs in which system

**Porch**: no-intake public tools and a trusted-person invitation.  
**Seven Days of Returning / Full Measure**: private, self-attested rest and useful action; no scores, streak penalties or invented witnessed Deeds.  
**Campfire**: stewardship interest, subject to separate consent, human review and no duty to serve.  
**Black Star × GRAFT**: *individual's private* evidence/provenance, never copied into common group records or Ask JAR;VIS.  
**reLATTE**: possible later *transport for public-safe consented process receipts*; a signed HOLD is not admission, a membership grant or permission to carry survivor narratives.  
**Watch the Watchmen**: prototype only. No independent ombuds authority, live confidential reporting or independent custody is yet established.

A proposed non-sensitive public return may record: **what generic process rule was tested; the reviewer-approved change; who owns the next action by explicitly volunteered role (if they agree to publication); stage = PROPOSED / HOLD / REVIEWED / FIELD_VERIFIED**. Do not put identities or sensitive stories into public GitHub or a shared ledger.

## Stop rule and forward rule

**STOP:** disclosures arrive where no qualified responder exists; a participant is identified without consent; a safety concern implicates the only reviewer; somebody asks for case adjudication; meeting confidentiality or withdrawal cannot be maintained. Stop the experiment, minimize retained data and seek suitable qualified human guidance.

**GO:** two or more genuinely independent adults can disagree safely; each consent is explicit; a tested process changes in response to review; people can leave; a small useful action is freely completed without creating a duty. Then contemplate a qualified, permissioned adult pilot.

**First chair is a promise to be accountable, not permission to collect a crowd.**
