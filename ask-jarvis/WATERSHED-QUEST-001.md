- Mobile-friendly, no account, back-end, GPS, maps API or partnership claim.
- Six imaginary Willow Run places. Observer, Storykeeper and Connector are available as simulation entry paths; Caretaker, Steward and Researcher are conditional fictional branches. Their native human prerequisites remain unfulfilled.

- Print a fictional quest and see a clearly hypothetical sponsor brief.
