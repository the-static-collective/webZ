# REAL-WORLDZ-001 / THE RIVER GATE

Independent public geospatial learning gateway. **No** City of Mankato, Minnesota DNR, MPCA, Blue Earth Project, park administrator or EcoMapper endorsement, data integration, cleanup permission or partnership is claimed.

## Working site
Open /real-worldz/ to select three approximate, coordinate-aligned Mankato park reference points: Sibley, Land of Memories, Riverfront. The illustration is a geolocated *point plot*, not a verified street map, route, river geometry or entrance. Visitors may opt to open OpenStreetMap externally. No third-party map tiles, scripts, geolocation, tracking, accounts, or cloud state were added.

Place cards expose source-linked information from original municipal and watershed institutions. The October 24 source snapshot says the Kern Bridge has a **scheduled** October 27, 2026 ceremony; the site is **not** a live bridge-access check.

Three ways in are Notice & Learn, Ask the Steward, and Take a Field Note. The first two generate fixed, reviewable questions; the third keeps freeform text in a local private download. No real volunteer task is assigned.

The source-only export uses schema real-worldz.source-only-invitation/v0, status HOLD_UNPUBLISHED. Every consent, work, witness, environmental safety, impact, GrO ACT, and Full Measure Deed claim is explicitly false. A fixed source-only question packet never includes private note text.

The optional Willow Run file input calls the existing original /labs/living-watershed/world.mjs importSimulation parser; fictional choices can inform a nonbinding interest suggestion but never confer real-world credits, permission, evidence, work, or environmental verdict.

## Human gates
Public place does not mean unrestricted access. Source references do not establish environmental conditions, a volunteer assignment, current opening status or a cleanup need. Bridge construction areas remain off limits until the responsible authority opens them. Do not enter water or handle risky materials for game points. All actual fieldwork is source-steward controlled and separately authorized.
