# WATERSHED-QUEST-001 / Fictional Willow Run Source

Status: **FICTIONAL SCENARIO — not actual environmental observations, licensed EcoMapper data, a verified steward, or partner authorization.**
This is the source-owned, published game-specimen whose revision identity can be checked in Git.

## Fictional place (not geocodable)
**Willow Run**, a wholly invented small watershed with a footbridge, a public path, a community table, a fictional cleanup staging area, and a closed scientific monitoring station. No real coordinates or identifiable people are presented.

## Issue
A fictional observer sees loose litter on a **public-facing path** beside a fictional creek. No contaminant, water-quality impairment, source of pollution, property boundary, cleanup permission, labor pledge, biological impact, or regulatory conclusion can be inferred from the observation.

## Invitation families

1. **THE FIRST LOOK — OBSERVER.** Try describing a fictional visible condition, its uncertainty, and the questions that need expert review. Do not collect anything or trespass.
2. **TELL THE RIVER'S STORY — STORYKEEPER.** Use imaginary observations to draft a clear educational caption, distinguishing fictional narrative, confirmed data, and unknowns.
3. **THREE CHAIRS — CONNECTOR.** Practice an opt-in invitation to a community conversation. No participant data, unsolicited outreach, or partner claim.
4. **THE CAREFUL PATH — CARETAKER.** A simulated, authorized, low-risk litter event. This is a *conditional future quest*: requires independently witnessed field orientation, a real host/land permission gate, hazard assessment, supplies and local waste rules. In the prototype all such claims are **not available**; fictional playback is explicitly a fictional what-if.
5. **COME BACK IN SPRING — STEWARD.** The longitudinal fictional return, conditional on separately reviewed work and relevant stewardship permissions. Gameplay may show a hypothetical future, never a real source verdict.
6. **THE QUESTION IN THE WATER — RESEARCHER.** Plan an educational question only; actual sampling requires a trained responsible institution, calibrated procedure, legal access, safety review, QA/QC and lab/data owner control. No sampling act is offered to visitors.

## Non-negotiable state distinctions
- Fictional simulation != actual observation.
- Quest proposal != accepted/authorized work.
- Source record != volunteer identity.
- Self-report != independent witness.
- Fictional human-review scene != verified human review.
- Stewardship contribution != rank, token, property right, donation, scientific efficacy, regulatory finding or environmental improvement.
- Declining, resting, reporting unsafe conditions, and not entering a hazardous scene are successful possible outcomes; no penalty.
- Observation review, site access, hazardous materials, water or shoreline conditions, consent, training, and minors require explicit human gates.
- The real Blue Earth Project EcoMapper is a separately operated system. No data, account, code, or API from it is used by this scenario.
- The production Abundent website is not changed by creating this source document.

## Participant experience
A mobile-first map-like tabletop of three or more fictional places. Choose a participation style, read local safety context, play a fictional consequence, and receive a printable or download-ready *simulation* diary. Advanced physical and scientific quests remain held in any real-world sense. Different paths are equally valued; visible consequences represent narrative history and new possibilities, not measured environmental improvement.
