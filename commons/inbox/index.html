LIVING RECEIVER 002 / PRIVATE INVITATION INBOX

Work can cross without taking over.

One community owns its inbox. Another can submit a proposed resource or method. Only invited reviewers can read and respond. Every report remains unverified until actual people confirm it.

Capability-link pilot. An invitation link grants access to whoever possesses it; it does not authenticate their identity or establish organizational permission. Do not use this for shelter casework, personal details, client names, contacts, home or shelter addresses, remittances or medical records. No real organizations are enrolled automatically.

04 / OWNER CONTROLS

Withdraw, rotate, or delete

Closing stops new submissions and new reviews; previously stored cards remain readable by invited reviewers. Rotating a key immediately invalidates the old reviewer or submitter invitation. Deleting removes the inbox, its cards, and its review history.
