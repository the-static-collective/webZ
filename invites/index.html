Not an attention trap.
Every path is valid. Doing something now is welcome, not compulsory. Passing an invitation doesn't enroll a friend. Declining creates no penalty. A new idea can grow as a fork without silently rewriting the original.


This first version is a link-carried network, not a hosted social feed. It cannot tell you who opened an invitation or guarantee that a reply reaches its creator. Future receiving and matching infrastructure must be explicitly permissioned before it is connected.
