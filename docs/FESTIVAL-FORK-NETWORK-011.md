**Actual design goal:** People do not merely learn what the founding architecture means; they help build the next cultural institution by selecting voluntary roles, shaping programming and choosing independently governed successors. The community may refuse the whole proposition. `ONE FESTIVAL != AUTHORITY_TO_PRODUCE_NEXT_FESTIVAL`.

There is no inherited permit or event license, no automatic key authority and no inherited membership between any of these events.\n\nThis is a human first-contact and community-power experiment, not an unqualified survivor-support group, political organizing operation, new crypto economy or existing campus/church/venue partnership. Independent community members—including those with little institutional access—have their own judgment and veto. Existing authorities may steward real infrastructure; they do not authorize other people's lives.

## Stage 1 — "Weird Worship Nights" as road-crew laboratories, not a membership gate

Our earlier **TABLE & ROAD / THE ROAD BRINGS THE FEAST** plan already connected traveling worship nights, potlucks, local service and an end-of-season festival. The essential portable production packet can travel from willing church to willing church, home-hosted dinner to community hall, or a consenting nonreligious site:

- One participatory **music set**, with songs/lyrics owned or licensed by actual authors and performers. Local worship can be explicitly Christian where the host chooses, but participation in faith activity **must never be a prerequisite** for the Commons, practical help or future events.
- One **open table**: inexpensive and accessible meal, printed public-safety-safe invitation, ASK/OFFER/BUILD/QUESTION cards. No public personal abuse disclosures or compulsory email/account.
- One **useful question** from a real local steward: what *already* works, what is missing, and what could be tried without disrupting current services?
- One **next-gathering invitation** that a new person can freely reject, adapt or independently host. No church owns the festival lineage merely by hosting a Road Night.
- One **portable work kit**: small PA/approved sound if permitted, artist/host permissions, timetable, optional music stems/click, printed cards and safe teardown checklist; only qualified crew use electricity/stages. Original Static Live/Song Garden/Suno components are optional candidate production organs, not rights/venue approval.
- An **ordinary nonreligious welcome and accessible alternative** at every openly shared Commons table. Families, different beliefs, disabilities and people without a church relationship must be able to join or decline without unequal treatment.

Some Road Nights may run as purely religious church events; if they do, **do not represent them as open public aid** or insist they become Commons events. The hosting community controls its own practice. The Commons invitation is voluntary and separable.

## Stage 4 — distributed stations actually compose the next festival

Instead of a centralized audience being asked to join a predesigned organization, a **walkable, accessible NEXT FESTIVAL FOUNDRY** offers independent stations that visitors can explore according to interest and time.

| Station | Real human work it could support | Owner control that never crosses automatically |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Road music & optional worship | New bands, portable worship nights, permitted public sets | Performer copyrights, host worship and secular-access decisions |
| Potluck & hospitality | Next table chain, inclusive food gathering | Food safety, host, participant and caregiver consent |
| Land & place care | Cleanup, gardens, protection of oaks, site stewardship | Landowner and environmental permission |
| Art, books & making | Posters, comics, zines, public-safe art | Artist, model and photographic rights |
| Useful work & repair | Repair skills, small practical jobs and demonstrations | Tool safety, actual owner permission and receiver confirmation |
| Family welcome & accessibility | Child-safe programming, quiet areas, language/access help | Qualified safeguards and family privacy |
| Institutional stewards & resources | Conversations about real space, food, program and equipment authority | Each organization retains its decision-making |
| Festival production & safety | Sound, staging, lighting, medical, logistics, funds, permits | Producer contracts, qualified operators and financing |
| Return & reality | Confirm what actually happened versus what was merely offered | Data minimization, subject consent, independent receiver reports |
| **NEXT FESTIVAL FOUND(R)Y** | Interested groups choose a new date, shape and geography | Separate new organizer, venue agreement, governance and budget |

There is no assumption that a station is staffed. Prospective event leaders must be real people who consent, not an AI-assigned roster. People may choose **30 minutes, 2 hours, a day, ongoing interest or no commitment**; the card is an **unsent preference**, not an offer of labor. A person can choose no station and still attend the festival if it happens. No contact info or private history is collected in the published demonstration. An event operator must seek independent consent and establish private, appropriate coordination later.

Each station could return one **public-safe proposal**: an actual piece of program needs, who controls it, what consent is required, how much it really costs and what would count as completion. Group decisions remain with actual participants.

## The creative twist: the October 2027 festival branches *during* September

At the conditional September 2027 Harmony Park Commons Homecoming, a participant can choose:

- **A — Harmony Park again:** a new locally led October or later proposal, not an extension of the September booking.
- **B — Somewhere else:** its own steward and site, with a new community's original rules.
- **A + B:** two distinct sets of proposals, operators, safety permissions and finance; joint shared method, **not** a joint automatically mandated organizer.
- **HOLD:** postpone, change season, or refuse. A "no" is a successful constitutional check.

A history of **Oaktoberfest, Oct 4–6 in 2019** appears in an [older Harmony Park calendar archive](https://hapar.wordpress.com/about/). That is evidence of a *historical* October event—not evidence of **October 2027** dates, camping availability, price, climate safety or permissions. Harmony Park's current [2026 events page](https://harmonypark.com/) shows spring and September dates and TBA events; it does **not** establish October 2027 availability.

**October occurs AFTER the six-month Voqal fellowship ends in September 2027.** A branch proposal can be an September fellowship output; **an actual October festival must secure independent organizers, venue permission, weather/camping plan and its own financing**. If time or conditions prohibit an October festival, a safe autumn one-day community table or 2028 full festival is a legitimate independently chosen successor.

## Executable, independently verifiable source-backed model

This draft adds a static [interactive station choice workshop](../bridge/first/festival-forks.html), source module [festival-forks.mjs](../bridge/first/festival-forks.mjs), and Node tests.

It imports the **original WebZ Suitcase `hash()`** and the original WebZ [RELATIONAL-INHERITANCE-008 method](../bridge/first/relational-inheritance.mjs); that cryptographic source-binding proves the method's **bytes**, not any person's identity or human agreement. It does **not** emit signed claims of real venue approval. A true independently signed event constitution would require actual separate human operator consent and original source Suitcase `SEED/GENESIS` packets, with independent verification and human safeguards. Nothing auto-books or sends.

The **fork generator** refuses invalid station/time/venue values, creates a distinct **HOLD** proposal for each of two selected venues, preserves the source method digest, emits no personally identifying data, and declares every machine/network/outreach/event effect **false**. This is a real local planning instrument. It does not establish a community, a festival or a partnership.

## Field falsifiers

- The event cannot be permitted, paid for or staffed safely → do not hold it.
- Road worship becomes the only route into community support → change the design to include nonreligious participation and separate service provision.
- People with institutional power dominate the festival while isolated participants cannot decide or exit → original social-justice goal has failed.
- Fork station participants are represented as volunteers without explicit agreement → reject and correct the roster.
- September venue booking is mistaken for October rights → reject October proposal.
- Two successor events share an undisclosed liability, unsafe custody chain or central veto → not independently governed.
- Nothing people actually do is independently confirmed by its receiver → report experiments, not an established economy.
