## The one radical question

Can a person without an existing network join a public, low-pressure activity, and then choose to help create **a second table** that does not depend on the founding group's permission, data, keys, programming or leader? This is the *social outcome to investigate*, not a result already achieved.

The first six-month April–September 2027 fellowship remains the Commons Circuit proposal: recurring in-person tables, voluntary potlucks/music and participant-owned activities; paid accessibility, local co-facilitation and independent safeguarding review; permission-bearing stewards; conditional Courthouse Park rehearsal and (only if properly produced and funded) Harmony Park finale. The $30,000 is unrestricted and proposed for human fieldwork, not new software. Venue, guests and festival budget remain independently unconfirmed; retain the honest fallback to a smaller closing gathering.

## Falsifiable human evaluation plan, not an established service

1. Ask compensated community members and qualified independent safety/accessibility reviewers to critique the paper first-door before any real first contact. Sensitive survivor support, if pursued, is a separately qualified pathway—never public festival intake.
2. Seek actual permission to host or join a first public table. No one needs a religious identity, phone, account or testimony to participate.
3. Observe and document non-sensitive, actual provider/recipient-confirmed useful contributions only with permission, separating offers, refusals and finished acts.
4. Invite, never manufacture, an independent second table. Its actual people choose the rules, location and records, and may decline to continue.
5. Test founder absence and useful continuity. **Failure condition:** the proposed successor cannot freely refuse, adapt, use local resources or continue without the originator.
6. Share public-safe findings only with independent review; record refusals and failure alongside success. Events/attendees are activity metrics, not proof of community power.

## Technical versus human evidence

The executable proof demonstrates that software receipts and separate signing keys can enforce a narrow **non-transitive digital authority** property in synthetic conditions. It does not establish human identity, legal governance, safe domestic-abuse support, valid consent, physical goods, an independently staffed gathering or any actual second community. An actual human-founded second table requires evidence outside code.

The media examples are **thought food**: one could borrow the grammar of a film, a folio, a record or a game to *ask a different question*. A source PR reference does not convey media rights or imply the source-owned media engine was executed by webZ.
