## Program: make the new culture visible, but keep power local

- **Music and storytelling stage:** local and emerging performers with explicit consent and rights to their material. Music is the invitation, not the membership test.
- **Potluck / community food commons:** an inclusive shared-food encounter **only with applicable venue, food-safety and service approvals**; use existing qualified vendors/caterers where preferable. No unsupported food-distribution claim.
- **Independent tables:** church/fellowship groups, campus/community practitioners, local food/artist/repair/transport groups, existing services and newly emerging circles can each request independent tables. **No partner is pre-enrolled**.
- **Matter & Idea Craft pavilion:** a public, hands-on space to make cards, comics, art and *non-sensitive* community planning artifacts. Paper-first; no reliance on a 3D printer, AI, NFTs or centralized registration.
- **Living Commons exchange:** permitted concrete offers/needs/work proposals and independently confirmed returned outcomes; no on-site financial token issuance, medical/legal case handling or public survivor intake.
- **Family spaces:** a quiet zone, accessible routes, age-appropriate music/play, caregivers remain responsible for children, identified child-safety point persons and policies. No public disclosure of child or survivor identities.
- **The inheritance ceremony:** willing community groups may read out a *public* statement of their own rules, publish a take-home editable method, and retain their own independent keys/records/authority. If no group freely chooses this, there is no staged or fabricated founding.

## The festival is the *human experiment*, not a marketing reward

The six-month method remains **one open table and one real stewardship conversation per week**, a chain of participant-owned potlucks and culture nights, with one mini-festival used for rehearsal of accessible entry, shared programming, safety, voluntary refusal, actually completed work and cleanup.

The September closing tests the stronger social question:
1. Can an invited family or newcomer attend and participate without an existing network, religious affiliation, account, coercion, testimony, or payment for ordinary support?
2. Have independent tables or stewards actually chosen to collaborate, keep separate records and govern their own activities?
3. Is any real useful exchange independently confirmed by the actual provider and recipient, rather than an intention displayed on a poster?
4. Can a willing local group carry the methods on after the original founder and fellowship funding leave?
5. Were paid care/access work and host costs handled responsibly, and were no-go decisions respected?

**Useful quantitative measures:** real host permissions; independent tables/producing contracts; event access accommodations used; actual cost and organizer hours; documented refusal/cancellation; actual receiver-confirmed tasks; voluntarily repeated circles; approved feedback without publishing identities. **Not** a headcount presented as belonging, a grant award presented as partnership, or an invitation presented as attendance.

## Permission/safety gate for an all-ages rural festival

Before any public announcement or ticketing: venue availability and signed terms; verified producer capability and financing; insurance and applicable permits; child-safeguarding and guardianship procedures; harassment prevention and reporting, qualified first aid/medical planning; accessible parking, quiet space, toilets, water, weather shelter, emergency/evacuation plan; stage/auditory safety, family-friendly programming and operating hours; food and vendor compliance; transparent refunds/fees; independent attendee/performer consent; data minimization; cleanup and stewardship of historic oaks and Lake Geneva setting.

Harmony Park's [published park rules](https://harmonypark.com/park-rules/) include no glass, no outside firewood, no pets at events except ADA service animals, limited vehicle access and specific noise limits. These must be honored; they do not replace actual event licensing or venue staff direction.
