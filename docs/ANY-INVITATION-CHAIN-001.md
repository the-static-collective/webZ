# ANY INVITATION CHAIN 001 — a possibility may travel

**Statement:** Invitations are not event RSVPs. They can carry an offer, question, need, capability, creative seed, borrowed tool, ride, repair, task, meal, invention, or an idea not yet classifiable. A person can act, offer, ask, pass it along unchanged, fork a new idea, or decline. All are valid terminal or continuation choices.

## Immediate proof

At `/invites/` on Abundent, a visitor writes a short public-safe possibility and chooses (or confirms) **NOW**, **SOON**, or **ANY TIME**. This creates a portable, no-account link. Another device can open that link and take six meaningful paths:

- **Act now** → choose an existing, actually implemented tool relevant to the topic; optional prepared response. No third-party work automatically begins.
- **Offer** → prepare a response referencing the specific invitation and share it voluntarily through the app where the link arrived.
- **Ask** → prepare a distinct question referencing the invitation.
- **Pass** → use device share/copy for the unchanged invitation, preserving the receiver's independent choice.
- **Fork** → make a derivative invitation with declared lineage; never rewrite the parent.
- **Decline** → simply leave; no record, penalty, or demand to explain.

Each invitation carries only its public-safe text, user-chosen availability, a random local link ID, and bounded ancestry. Browser URL fragments carry the content; it is not submitted to Abundent, and a receiving person must explicitly choose to share or send any reply. Link contents are visible to anyone holding the link. No contact graph is harvested. No automatic friend-of-friend messages. No public feed.

**The invitation link is not a signature.** Its `id` and `parentId` are self-declared; do not treat them as reLATTE authenticated crossings, genuine permissions, verified provenance, observed action, or commitment. Link-fork ancestry is intentionally descriptive, not authoritative.

## Where it stops

This is **a functional link-carried network**, not yet a hosted social network. It has no accounts, persisted shared history, subscriber graph, server-delivered replies, notifications, authentication, verified matching, or receipt backchannel. Replies can be manually shared through the same app/conversation that carried the invite, or saved locally; delivery to the original inviter is not guaranteed. These limitations are explicitly shown.

The immediate action door may open LIVE OFFER 001 or FIRST CIRCLE when relevant. Those tools have their own consent and evidence boundaries. The invitation is not silently transported into them.
