# WINTER WARMTH 001 — Firewood Commons / field protocol

**October 28, 2026 | proposal and local-only draft tool; no operating bank, stocked warehouse, recipients, agreements, deliveries or enrolled volunteers.**

## Why this exists

Heat urgency cannot wait for green wood to season. Treat **existing, tested-dry, safely sourced stock** and **newly cut future stock** as different inventories with different deadlines. A standing dead tree does not prove the fuel is dry, free of pests or legally movable. This is a *warmth lane* connected to the Garden, Jubilee revival, Common Ground, local fuel vendors, and existing energy-relief agencies, not a conversion ministry or student recruitment program.

## Ordered field flow

1. **Immediate relief referral first.** For household heat urgency, contact a real assistance provider. Minnesota Energy Assistance can include payments to wood fuel providers: https://mn.gov/commerce/energy/consumer-assistance/energy-assistance-program/ . Waseca/Blue Earth and other counties: Minnesota Valley Action Council, 507-345-6822, https://www.mnvac.org/programs/energy/energy-assistance-eap-2/. Minnesota Commerce announced 2026–27 funding October 26; MVAC's page still carried a funding-delay note as of October 28. Verify current payment and crisis availability with the provider, not this site.
2. **Supply classification.** Identify legally owned, available, already split wood; whether wood is seasoned, freshly cut, diseased or uncertain; county of origin; species; true moisture reading from a newly split face; intended delivery county. A known dry tested sample is a *lead*, not permission to move.
3. **Hazard screen.** Tree felling, hanging limbs, power lines and storm-damaged material are for qualified appropriately insured professionals. No untrained volunteers or minors doing chainsaw work. Check landowner/tree owner's authority, municipal permits, professional equipment, and vehicle/cargo requirements.
4. **Pest and environmental screen.** Follow current MDA quarantine rules and any county restrictions. Prefer truly local wood. Uncertified hardwood firewood cannot be moved out of quarantined areas. MDA-certified, properly labeled heat-treated wood is the safest legal travel option where rules permit. Some infected wood must not be distributed. https://www.mda.state.mn.us/plants-insects/firewood-information
5. **Heating quality.** EPA advises wood moisture below 20%, appropriate dry seasoning, correct appliance and annual chimney inspections, and smoke/CO detectors. Green or contaminated wood is not a substitute for heat. https://www.epa.gov/burnwise/burn-wise-facts-figures-health-and-safety-tips
6. **Consent and delivery.** An established, locally accountable assistance agency or wood bank stewards private need; donor-to-private-home direct matching is not published. Independent receiver may HOLD, REFUSE or accept separately. Confirm transport insurance, appropriate safe storage, access and recipient choices.
7. **A truthful return.** Distinguish a source lead, a donor statement, an inspected inventory, an approved match, a shipped load and an independently confirmed delivery. Report no identifying recipient details without separately informed consent. No tokens, reputation scores or conditional spiritual participation.

## Proposed sources of capacity — no partnership claims

- Homeowners with safe yard wood and landowner permission; tree-care firms with proper insurance; municipal tree-waste programs (only with permission and pest regulations); local farmers and approved dry-storage sites.
- Churches and voluntary civic groups: seasonal volunteer coordination, shelter and outreach, community food events, possible covered storage. No aid requires worship.
- Colleges and Extension: adult logistics, species/disease research, moisture testing, weatherization education and route design with their own approval.
- K–12: optional school-approved environmental education or public-awareness materials; **never child-to-household matching or hazardous wood work**.
- Existing agencies/fuel vendors: paid or donated *legitimate* wood-fuel supply and energy-assistance routing. Do not reinvent crisis intake.

## Reusable one-week experiment

- Day 1: find **one** accountable steward and ask if the need exists and whether they want help. They may decline.
- Day 2: identify **one** proposed source of dry fuel; verify ownership and condition without publicity.
- Day 3: MDA origin/destination and municipal permission check; insurance/transport check.
- Day 4: choose safe storage or licensed supplier routing, not a random household handoff.
- Day 5: steward independently evaluates proposal; refusal/unknown recorded accurately.
- Day 6: if all gates cleared, deliver one approved unit or complete an authorized improvement; else HOLD.
- Day 7: report only what actually occurred, with permission and no private household identification.

**Source precedes permission, permission precedes movement, movement precedes a receipt.** No direct submissions exist on the public app. The on-page composer produces unsent public-safe text only.
