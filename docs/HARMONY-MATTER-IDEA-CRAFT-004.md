# HARMONY / MATTER × IDEA CRAFT 004

**2026-10-29 · source-owned composition experiment · synthetic, local, no effects.**

## Thesis

A real resource and a living idea can both be **particulars** with attributable histories. They cannot be accounted for identically.

- **Matter** has location, physical custody, units, transformation inputs/outputs, conservation constraints and competing uses. A claim of 42 WOOD, conflicting fictional observation of 39, and proposed 4 WOOD -> ASH / HEAT cannot create any recognized spendable inventory or physically burn anything without independent evidence. Byproducts are deliberately UNKNOWN, never mathematically invented.
- **Ideas** can generate many versions (song, film, photographs, manga panels, booklet, live performance, world) while the source persists. The source's *lineage* can be addressed; a copy's possession doesn't grant copyright, exclusivity, a patent, or an authorized commercial license.
- **Craft** relates input particulars to an intended descendant. The specific relation may be material consumption, nonconsumptive expression, mixed asset assembly, teaching, license, loan, stewardship or transfer. An economic representation may be useful without being money, a securitized claim or speculative NFT.
- **Human usefulness** is downstream of actual use, testimony and independently witnessed deeds—not a number awarded for producing a hash or writing a prompt.

## One runnable cut

Open `/bridge/harmony/craft.html` on HTTPS or localhost. It uses Web Crypto SHA-256 to give an exact local proposal a content address; `composeMatterIdeaCraft(options)` in `craft-economy.mjs` is a pure module, exportable to other tools. Default inputs: **fictional 42 WOOD claimed, fictional 39 observed, 4 proposed to transform, one cultural idea, one selected expression**. The output contains two source objects, two separately addressed transformations, lineage edges, the other six possible cultural expression formats, a proposed mixed physical/cultural story kit and explicitly zero real effects.

The UI can export a standalone JSON file. No input is transmitted, stored in a database, signed, uploaded, minted or posted. Local SHA-256 establishes only integrity for the recorded bytes; not an author signature or a confirmed human event. Published UI code is safe; exported topic text becomes public only if the operator deliberately shares the file.

## No fake NFTs, no compulsory prices

A **nonfungible record** is a uniquely attributable item/occurrence, not automatically a token. If someone wants optional public-chain ERC-721 or similar interoperability, that requires separately verified permissions, rights statement, custody/transfer semantics, licensing, moderation, consumer protection and an explicit receiver acceptance. Minting a token does not establish a copyright transfer, a claim on material, royalties, or marketplace value. Do not lock basic cultural participation behind token purchases.

A finished cultural asset could later become usable as *an authorized licensed performance, printable edition, visual lesson, host-local tradition, or contracted contribution*. It becomes a real economic asset only in a relevant locality with separately evidenced use rights or custody. The demo is **not** claiming that such rights or value exist now.

## Acceptance gates for a real crossing

1. Physical: independently authorized steward and actual inventory/custody with bounded ownership and safety.
2. Creative: source author/rights holder, permissible inputs, output bytes, independent release approval, exact source and output hashes.
3. Full Measure: actual authorized human contribution with a separate recipient/host witness.
4. PENNY: source-owned confirmed work/custody policy and legally appropriate claims; never zero-evidence minting.
5. reLATTE: explicit signed offer, receiver-local HOLD/REFUSE/ADMIT, no implicit transport authority.
6. THE BELT: external settlement separate from typed interior accounting, no invented exchange rate or payment.
7. Accessibility and consent: nothing forces people to accept a token or join an economic ledger to share cultural work.
