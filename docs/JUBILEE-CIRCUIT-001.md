# Jubilee Circuit 001 — The Road Brings the Feast
**Date: 2026-10-28. Status: proposed cultural field kit, not an enrolled or operational partner network.**

## Claim
A festival can assemble a temporary village. A church or civic neighbor can help sustain work across seasons. A community facing homelessness or material scarcity knows its own priorities. Music carries relationships, invitations, shared memory and cultural exchange between them. **Different voices. Shared repertoire. Local authority.**

## First proposed crossing
Southern Minnesota ↔ Ghana, beginning with one music seed and one locally authorized, publicly describable organizational task. Healer of Nations' reported conversations with Ghanaian homelessness representatives are a *lead*, not an enrolled partner or proof of consent. No Minnesota festival or congregation has agreed to host this pilot here. No first song, need, delivery, or independently confirmed achievement is claimed.

## Song and action loop
1. Local creator offers a seed on explicit terms: permission to record, distribute, perform, remix, translate, credit and compensate (where applicable). No default license.
2. A receiving community may respond musically or decline. Distinct traditions should not be homogenized for branding.
3. A locally authorized steward selects a safe organizational need, with privacy and refusal rights.
4. Other participants offer concrete services, supplies, research, music, money or facilities. **OFFER is not ACCEPTED; ACCEPTED is not DELIVERED; DELIVERED is not VERIFIED USEFUL.**
5. Independent steward confirms outcomes; publish only consented, non-sensitive methods and recordings.
6. The next community may carry the next song and choose its own act. The initial recipient is not trapped in the role of beneficiary.

## Two-hour field table
00–15 welcome and food; 15–35 music and optional answer; 35–55 one authorized public need; 55–90 one bounded useful task; 90–110 verify what happened; 110–120 invite next voice. The /jubilee/ page prints as a one-sheet run-of-show.

## Stewardship and safety gates
- Local receiving community chooses needs, approves use of resources and may withdraw. Do not centralize authority, claim shelter occupants as partners or replace their leadership.
- Food, shelter-related help and other material support are **unconditional**. Worship participation, music and storytelling are optional.
- Never publish sensitive names, personal cases, medical/immigration histories, minors' data, exact shelter locations or addresses.
- Compare local procurement, locally buildable knowledge and distant shipment using total costs including maintenance, customs, shipping and disposal. Never send unsafe or unusable e-waste.
- Ensure appropriate permissions, technical/food safety, accessibility, safeguarding and host/venue authority.
- Keep author/performer ownership and credit. Do not promise royalty-free remixes, translations or archival rights without consent.
- The static invitation composer sends nothing; mailto opens the user's own email app only after inspection. It is not a database, account, signup, matching system, or reLATTE crossing.

## Candidate adapters (not implemented here)
Static-live: music/radio; webZ: public invitation; Porchlight Commons: organization-level need comparison; Garden/BananaSpork: help slips; reLATTE: signed authorized crossing/HOLD/ADMIT/REFUSE; JAR-VIS: prototypes; GRAFT: reusable methods; Full Measure: optional return and habit practices. Each retains its own law. Cross-linking does not mean integration is live.

## 30-day field plan and proof threshold
- Week 1: find one willing host and one independently identified local steward; if Ghana approval is not available, run a Minnesota-only gathering and label it accordingly.
- Week 2: agree on one consented song and public organizational task, confirm permissions and compare local pathways.
- Week 3: gather for two hours, complete or honestly decline a bounded act, distinguish offers from verified delivery.
- Week 4: recipient/steward confirms useful impact (or not), and creator approves any public music/reuse. Preserve learning and failure.
- **Pass** requires willing host, independently authorized recipient steward, creator-permitted music, completed useful act, independent result acknowledgment. Otherwise label **PARTIAL** or **UNKNOWN**.

Music can travel farther than a truck; it cannot override a receiver's decision.
