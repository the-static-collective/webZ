## Public promise

Abundent is an open community initiative. Its public voice is present-tense and operational: people bring needs, offers, questions, art, and skills; willing humans choose what to do; the result is returned with its limits and source intact.

Do not use invented founding dates, years of service, staff lists, tax status, credentials, membership totals, funders, sponsor seals, formal church affiliations or partner endorsements. Presence is earned by **working openly now**, not by claiming undocumented history.

## Four honest program states

- **The Garden / food access — IN FORMATION.** The Waseca cooperative grocery effort is independently organized. Abundent can support community experiments but does not speak for the store or its members.
- **Care & Return — SEEKING STEWARDS.** Voluntary peer connection for isolated domestic-abuse survivors and family-system survivors remains a developing effort. No confidential public intake, emergency response, legal representation, or clinical practice.
- **Music & Culture — PUBLIC WORK.** The Static Collective's music and publishing output exists publicly; collaborations, artist permissions, and royalties remain particular to each work.
- **Open Workbench — OPEN EXPERIMENTS.** Source code and local demonstrations exist; deployment and operational capability must be independently verified.

## The actual participation loop

1. **Notice** an explicitly stated need, opportunity or public question.
2. **Offer** an appropriately bounded contribution, without treating the offer as permission.
3. **Choose** a willing steward and smallest testable next step.
4. **Do** the work with owners' permission and without exposing personal records.
5. **Return** a report of what actually happened, naming what remains unverified.

When an approval or moderation bottleneck appears, **invite another willing steward** to hold that role. Do not bypass review.

## Public communications rules

- Write in a natural plural when the endeavor is shared, but never invent actual team members.
- Publish the *work* and the actual invitation; label incubating services accurately.
- Make at least one real thing possible today (a conversation, contribution, reviewed card, music listen or repository inspection).
- No public intake of survivor stories or private evidence on an unverified platform.
- Human trust, consent, refusal, and safety outrank expanding the platform.
- The Commons page links to connected public surfaces; it does not grant authority in their names.

## Follow-through: first three weeks

**Week 1 — Door and first steward:** Publish this page after review, test external links and the question desk on a phone, recruit one willing human to handle one bounded inquiry, prepare a consent-appropriate answer/redirect process.

**Week 2 — One documented useful result:** Record an opt-in, non-sensitive need → offer → action → return. Distinguish public evidence from claims and redact names unless authorized.

**Week 3 — Repeatable rhythm:** Establish a small weekly field note: what happened, what is open, what is blocked, what kind of steward is needed next. Add a private contact path for sensitive survivor work *only after* suitable safeguards and humans exist.
