
# PORCHLIGHT COMMONS 001 — Global Crossing / Minnesota ↔ Ghana

**2026-10-28. Status: source-built public demonstration; real field participation not established.**

The first deliverable is a lightweight, printable, exportable, **browser-only** organizational need card at [webZ/commons](../commons/). It compares three possible means of satisfying a bounded request:

1. **Local purchase:** strengthen local suppliers rather than assume donated imports are cheaper.
2. **Distant supply:** evaluate lawful, functional equipment/food transfer only with landed costs, compliance and local suitability.
3. **Knowledge transfer:** share a verified, adaptable practice rather than moving physical equipment.

This is *resource/idea arbitrage* between organizations, **not a marketplace for displaced people or personal case files**.

## Origin and first potential cross
- **Ghana:** Healer of Nations is reportedly speaking with homelessness community leaders; a capital-intensive food shelf, energy/heat, and communication are the currently described needs. These statements are user-reported, not an agreement, verified partner identity or service-ready site. Location spelling and site suitability require direct local confirmation.
- **Minnesota:** Porchlight-001 proposes asking one existing provider about one provider-approved non-sensitive task. Research into public volunteer doors has been done; no Minnesota provider is onboarded.
- **The useful crossover:** compare locally sourced shelf-stable parcels vs US shipments; test know-how for solar charging, food-preservation heat banks, refurbishment and maintenance; keep e-waste and hazardous/unsafe equipment out of export streams.

## Operational sequence

```text
local leader names a problem
 -> authorized provider chooses public organizational description
 -> independent lived-experience review, with power to object
 -> HOLD card + bounded resource/know-how comparisons
 -> locally permitted offer and explicit partner admission
 -> logistics and safety (food handling, land, power, customs)
 -> helper reports delivered work
 -> independent recipient/operator confirms quantity and quality
 -> actual cost + outcome receipt
 -> return a re-usable method, only with local permission
```

The web page implements **only the HOLD-card draft and conditional cost comparison**; no verified exchange, imports, dispatch, partner enrollment, local database, or authority claims. It intentionally stores nothing and transmits nothing. No personal records, coordinates or names should be typed into draft fields; lightweight input filtering is **not** a substitute for human redaction.

## Proposed 30-day field experiment
- **Week 1:** Healer's existing leaders select one food need and local steward; Minnesota provider selects one permitted volunteer/supply task. No client records leave either organization.
- **Week 2:** Verify Ghanaian local procurement quotes, donated resources and possible methods. Ask local reviewers whether aid would reduce workload. Establish explicit refuse/withdraw paths.
- **Week 3:** Using existing partners, complete one reversible, safe, low-value task under their procedures. No speculative shipment or equipment import.
- **Week 4:** Authorized local operator and recipient independently review actual receipt, reliability, costs, privacy and whether to repeat.

## First proof and stop conditions

**Success** is one locally approved task leading to one independently verified practical outcome plus honest costs and permission to share only non-sensitive learning.

**STOP** for no local owner, coerced participation, privacy exposure, unsafe food/power practices, missing permission, improper e-waste exports, increased staff burden or false confirmation.

## What must not be inferred
`draft != partner approval`; `public card != request for shelter placement`; `offer != available stock`; `lowest stated cost != best option`; `reported != confirmed`; `software passed != field proof`.
