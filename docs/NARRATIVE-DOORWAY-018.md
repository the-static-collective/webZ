## Four claim classes and the forbidden shortcuts

| Class | What it can mean | What it does not mean |
|---|---|---|
| WITNESS | Preserved source with carrier, dates, provenance and uncertainty | A lyric, memoir or official record is automatically complete truth. |
| POSSIBLE | Proposed future, fictional institution or diegetic design | Fiction already happened or was prophesied. |
| MADE | Prototype, print object or executed source-native fixture | A synthetic test establishes food delivery, consent, safety or shared governance. |
| ENCOUNTER | Independently willing human response, including refusal | Sending an invitation is endorsement or participation. |

A newly noticed connection between old sources can have a new birthday; it does not give either original source foreknowledge.
