## Measurement: keep denominators honest

**Proposed first milestone:** 50 *measured* unique visits, 10 actual completed first actions, 3 voluntary reshares, 1 independently acknowledged useful real-world outcome. These are targets, **not** observed values.

Track aggregates by channel. `utm_source=personal|community|church|foodnetwork|organizer`, `utm_medium=direct|social`, `utm_campaign=circulation_001` may help an existing analytics setup separate sources, but **UTMs do not create analytics**. Do not put recipient identities, email addresses, or raw needs into query parameters.

Definitions:
- **Visit:** observed from an actual configured analytics system, not an impression or a sent message. If unavailable, mark **UNKNOWN**; never claim zero traffic from absence of measurement.
- **First action:** a person demonstrably completes a useful page action, not merely loads a page or creates another invitation.
- **Voluntary reshare:** known intentional share, not a system-created suggestion or a click.
- **Verified outcome:** separate acknowledgment from an actual recipient/steward after a bounded real-world act. Generated cards, self-reports, and synthetic fixtures do not qualify.
- **Refusal/failure:** preserve as legitimate data. No incentives for involuntary recruitment.

Suggested review fields: date; channel; measured visits (or unknown); first actions; reshares; independent outcomes; broken handoffs; next repair. Aggregate only: do not collect names or sensitive stories.
