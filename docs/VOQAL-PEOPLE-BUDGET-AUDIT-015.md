## The budget insight

**An important person's time is not a thing we automatically have to buy.** Start with a 15–25 minute informational request or existing free public gathering. Pay the real costs of food, travel and accessibility when incurred; **pay people for actual substantial labor** (especially people asked to co-design from lived experience and independent accessibility/safety specialists). Do not buy official permission, access, policy decisions, testimony or endorsements. A meal is hospitality, not an agreement. In every case ask the receiver what they need and whether organizational gift/conflict rules apply.

**Four price classes:** (1) $0: short introductions, existing public gatherings, information, reciprocal invitation; (2) direct costs: coffee, basic meal or mileage, not a fee for status; (3) paid labor: resident-designed sessions, interpreting, accessibility and safeguarding consultation; (4) separately quoted and authorized event costs. No expense is booked merely because it appears below.

## Meeting sequence: the budget follows the actual demand

- **Listening pair (week 1–2 after selection):** 1 resident/actual participant + 1 food or community organizer. Use a public/free room; only incur food/transit if real. Outcome: one specific priority, or refusal. Do not turn it into a support-services intake.
- **Capability pair (week 3–4):** an operations steward (e.g. food-shelf or co-op) + a local maker/host who can authorize an experiment. Seek a single reversible no-data action, with explicit independent consent. No honorarium to an institutional officer for their permission.
- **Second Table trial (after co-design):** two independently organized groups with a participant-selected host. Only count success if an actual resident can refuse, change rules, and continue without the founder; source-signed synthetic keys cannot prove that.
- **Review table:** independently compensated safeguarding/access expertise and resident co-designers; publish only public-safe lessons they approve. First table always has a no-account/no-religious-obligation route; faith gatherings and optional art/music stations remain opt-in.
