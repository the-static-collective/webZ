The obvious next experiment

Start with a neighborhood's existing hands.

Don't pick between “no store” and “full store” just yet. Learn what can work producer-direct, with existing licensed businesses and routes, while the co-op pursues its own feasibility study. Ope, there's a practical pilot right there.

Three lanes / never blur them

One town, three distinct kinds of work.

01 / COMMERCE

Grow & sell

Licensed sellers, producer-direct orders, storefronts, pricing, inventory and payment. Each seller controls its own goods and records.

02 / CARE

Rescue & share

Food shelves, donation channels and food-rescue partners use their own eligibility, safeguarding and food-handling rules. Aid is not commerce.

03 / COMMONS

Meet & learn

OFFER / NEED / BUILD / QUESTION cards, introductions, consented referrals, one small accepted task, and honest reports. An idea is not a commitment.

What if we help those parts work together now, then let real demand show us what building we actually need? No takeover, no new middleman. Just one useful pilot. Don'tcha think, though?”
